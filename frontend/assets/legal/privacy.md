# VoiceOps — Privacy Policy
*Last updated: [date] — Demo build*

**What we collect:**
- **Voice audio** — captured while you talk to the co-rider assistant, sent
  to our speech-processing provider (AssemblyAI) to understand your
  requests and generate spoken replies.
- **Location data** — your GPS position while on shift, used to calculate
  routes, ETAs, and match you to nearby delivery orders.
- **Delivery and account data** — your name, contact info, and the
  deliveries assigned to you, stored to run the service.

**How we use it:**
- To provide the core app functionality (voice assistant, navigation,
  order matching, delivery tracking).
- To notify customers of estimated arrival times.
- We do not sell your data to third parties.

**Who we share it with:**
- AssemblyAI (voice processing), our routing/traffic data provider, and
  Twilio (customer SMS notifications) — only as needed to run the features
  above, not for their own marketing use.
