Talk to your operations. Let your operations talk back.

Kora is a voice-first co-rider for last-mile drivers. Say what you need, and it works across routes, orders and customers while you keep your eyes on the road.

The gap

Every tap is a glance off the road.

Without Kora

One stop, six screen tasks

  • Find the next address
  • Check the route
  • Call the customer
  • Update the stop
  • Accept a new order
  • Count what's left

Simple at a desk. On a bike, scooter or in a car, each one competes with the road.

With Kora

One sentence, one answer

You“Kora, what's next?”
Kora“Stop 9, Alex K., 12 Central Avenue. Nine minutes. The route is on your map.”

Hands on the bars. Eyes on the road. The work happens anyway.
